    Who We Are

    "Every job we take is a promise to show up on time, every time."

    Uroh Emmanuel, popularly known as D-General, founded D-General Transport Consult after graduating from Lagos State University. What began as executive bus hire for events around Lagos has grown into a full transport and logistics operation — interstate delivery, convoy coordination, vehicle leasing, NYSC travel and car rental all run under the same standard the business started with.

    That growth wasn't about chasing every opportunity. Each new service was added because clients kept asking for it — a church that booked a coaster for a retreat later needed convoy coordination for a bigger event; a family that rented a car for the weekend later needed help sourcing a vehicle to buy outright. The business grew in the direction its clients pointed it.

    Today, D-General operates across Lagos and nationwide, with over 3,500 services delivered and a reputation built less on advertising and more on people calling back the next time they needed to move.
